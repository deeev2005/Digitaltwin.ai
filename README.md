# 🏭 TwinFlow — Digital Twin & Industrial Intelligence Platform

TwinFlow is an interactive prototype designed to visually demonstrate how physical manufacturing operations translate into real-time digital twins and predictive intelligence.

---

## 📌 Overview

TwinFlow demonstrates the end-to-end lifecycle of smart manufacturing data:
$$\text{Physical Production Line} \rightarrow \text{IoT Signals} \rightarrow \text{Digital Twin} \rightarrow \text{AI Intelligence} \rightarrow \text{Predictive Actions} \rightarrow \text{Operator Decisions}$$

### Key Modules:
- **Digital Twin View:** Real-time visual representation of vehicle assemblies and station sensors.
- **Intelligence Engine:** AI-driven anomaly detection, root-cause analysis, and predictive maintenance alerts.
- **Operations & Traceability:** In-depth unit tracking across stamping, body shop, paint, and final assembly lines.
- **Validation & Simulation:** Interactive simulation of sensor variances, drift, and stress testing.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** [React 19](https://react.dev/) + [TanStack Start](https://tanstack.com/start) / [TanStack Router](https://tanstack.com/router)
- **Bundler & Build Tool:** [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS v4](https://tailwindcss.com/) + [Radix UI](https://www.radix-ui.com/)
- **Charts & Visualizations:** [Recharts](https://recharts.org/) & [Lucide Icons](https://lucide.dev/)
- **State & Data Management:** [TanStack Query](https://tanstack.com/query)
